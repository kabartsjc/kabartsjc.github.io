About

I lead courses in Cyber Security Systems Engineering, Secure Software Engineering and Cyber Risk Analysis at George Mason University, and chair the undergraduate program in Cybersecurity Engineering, where I am leading a redesign of the BS curriculum around Entrepreneurial Mindset Learning and ABET outcomes.

My research turns threat modeling into something operators can act on. I created the Cyber-ARGUS framework to measure the operational impact of cyber events, first for ADS-B scenarios; it was published in the Journal of Network and Computer Applications and became the basis of the DALNIM project. I co-invented ADS-BSec, a patented framework for securing aircraft surveillance broadcasts.

Before returning to academia I was Deputy Director of Research and Development at the Brazilian Airspace Control Institute, ICEA/DECEA, working with the FAA, ANAC, Boeing and SAAB, and leading the first Brazilian certification processes for ATC systems. I retired from the Brazilian Air Force as a Lieutenant Colonel and founded GML Tecnologias, which supported early Brazilian RPAS approvals and the EDP smart grid design.

Research

Primary areas

R-01 · AGENTIC AI

Trust assurance for agentic and LLM-based systems

Provenance scoring, action risk gating and decision-assurance layers for AI agents that plan, act and coordinate, including MCP-based tool ecosystems and multi-agent drones.

R-02 · AVIATION

Aviation cybersecurity

GNSS jamming and spoofing impact, ADS-B authentication and anomaly detection, and the security and privacy of UAS Traffic Management.

R-03 · MISSION

Cyber mission impact assessment

Semantic and Bayesian models that map cyber assets to the missions they support, so commanders and operators can see what an attack actually costs.
